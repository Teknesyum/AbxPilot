## 8.6 Renal Impairment

> 8.6 Renal Impairment Reductions in systemic elimination as well as urinary excretion of mecillinam are anticipated with decreases in renal function. The clinical significance of these changes on efficacy is unknown. The available safety information does not suggest a need for dosage adjustment [see Clinical Pharmacology ( 12.3 )] .
