# ampicillin: Renal Dosing Evidence

## 1. US label (no renal dosing in Dosage and Administration)

Source: Ampicillin for Injection (WG Critical Care), US prescribing information
Link: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=38ec7b7a-9caf-45f2-88a1-0f78f089a1e4
Label effective date: 2026-06-22
Text: openFDA label JSON, verbatim. The only renal dosing sentence is in Overdosage:

### openFDA field: overdosage

In patients with renal function impairment, ampicillin-class antibacterials can be removed by hemodialysis but not peritoneal dialysis.

## 2. UK SmPC section 4.2

Source: Ampicillin 500 mg powder for solution for injection (Chemidex Pharma Ltd), UK SmPC
Link: https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/12892/smpc
SmPC date of revision: August 2024
Text: quoted verbatim from the emc SmPC page by a page-extraction tool; re-check against the page before sign-off.

"In the presence of severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance <10ml/min) a reduction in dose or extension of dose interval should be considered."

"In cases of dialysis, an additional dose should be administered after the procedure."

Section 4.4: "Dosage should be adjusted in patients with renal impairment (see section 4.2)."
