## UK SmPC 1 — 4.2

> The dosage should be reduced if renal function is markedly impaired.

## UK SmPC 1 — 4.2 Special dosage

> Special dosage: The elimination of phenoxymethylpenicillin potassium is reduced in case of renal insufficiency. The dose interval should be adjusted to every 8 hours to 12 hours according to the severity of renal impairment. Dosage adjustment may be necessary in patients with impaired liver function when they also have renal failure. In this situation the liver may be a major excretion route.

## UK SmPC 2 — 4.2 Renal impairment

> The dosage should be reduced if renal function is markedly impaired.
