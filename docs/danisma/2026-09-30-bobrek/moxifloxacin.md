## Dosage and Administration (highlights bullet)

> • No dosage adjustment in patients with renal or hepatic impairment.

## 8.6 Renal Impairment

> Renal Impairment The pharmacokinetic parameters of moxifloxacin are not significantly altered in mild, moderate, severe, or end-stage renal disease. No dosage adjustment is necessary in patients with renal impairment, including those patients requiring hemodialysis (HD) or continuous ambulatory peritoneal dialysis (CAPD) [see Dosage and Administration (2) and Clinical Pharmacology (12.3) ] .
