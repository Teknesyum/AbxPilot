## 2.4 Dosage in Adult Patients With Renal Impairment (Table 1)

> 2.4 Dosage in Adult Patients With Renal Impairment In adult patients with renal impairment (creatinine clearance less than or equal to 40 mL/min) and dialysis patients (hemodialysis and CAPD), the intravenous dose of ZOSYN should be reduced based on the degree of renal impairment. The recommended daily dosage of ZOSYN for patients with renal impairment administered by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes is described in Table 1 . Table 1: Recommended Dosage of ZOSYN in Patients with Normal Renal Function and Renal Impairment (As total grams piperacillin and tazobactam) Administer ZOSYN by intravenous infusion over 30 minutes. Creatinine clearance, mL/min All Indications (except nosocomial pneumonia) Nosocomial Pneumonia Greater than 40 mL/min 3.375 grams every 6 hours 4.5 grams every 6 hours 20 mL/min to 40 mL/min Creatinine clearance for patients not receiving hemodialysis 2.25 grams every 6 hours 3.375 grams every 6 hours Less than 20 mL/min 2.25 grams every 8 hours 2.25 grams every 6 hours Hemodialysis 0.75 g (0.67 g piperacillin and 0.08 g tazobactam) should be administered following each hemodialysis session on hemodialysis days 2.25 grams every 12 hours 2.25 grams every 8 hours CAPD 2.25 grams every 12 hours 2.25 grams every 8 hours For patients on hemodialysis, the maximum dose is 2.25 g every twelve hours for all indications other than nosocomial pneumonia and 2.25 g every eight hours for nosocomial pneumonia. Since hemodialysis removes 30% to 40% of the administered dose, an additional dose of 0.75 g ZOSYN (0.67 g piperacillin and 0.08 g tazobactam) should be administered following each dialysis period on hemodialysis days. No additional dosage of ZOSYN is necessary for CAPD patients.

## 8.6 Renal Impairment

> 8.6 Renal Impairment In patients with creatinine clearance ≤ 40 mL/min and dialysis patients (hemodialysis and CAPD), the intravenous dose of ZOSYN should be reduced to the degree of renal function impairment [see Dosage and Administration (2) ] .
