## 1. DSÖ AWaRe Antibiyotik Kitabı (2022)

- Künye: World Health Organization. *The WHO AWaRe (Access, Watch, Reserve) antibiotic book*.
  Geneva: WHO; 2022. ISBN 978-92-4-006238-2 (elektronik). Lisans CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO.
- URL: https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/365237
- Yayın tarihi (IRIS kaydı): 2022-12-09
- Bölüm: "HOSPITAL FACILITY — 24. Sepsis in adults (including septic shock)", s. 295-318.
  Tablo 24.7, s. 314-317.

### Tablo Başlığı

> "Table 24.7 – Empiric antibiotic treatment for community-acquired sepsis of bacterial origin in adults"

> "Where more than one antibiotic is recommended for an infection, they are listed in alphabetical order and they should be considered equal treatment options, unless otherwise indicated."

### Kaynağı Bilinmeyen Klinik Sepsis Satırı (Birebir)

> "Clinical sepsis of unknown origin(a)
> Cefotaxime(b) (IV): 2 g given every 8 hours
> OR
> Ceftriaxone(b) (IV): 2 g given once a day
> COMBINED WITH
> Amikacin(c) (IV): 15 mg/kg given once a day
> OR
> Gentamicin(c) (IV): 5 mg/kg given once a day"

Süre sütunu:

> "7 days. But duration depends on the patient's underlying disease, the degree of immunosuppression, the causative pathogen (if any identified later on) and clinical progression)"

Dipnotlar:

> "a If the source of the infection is determined please follow infection-specific guidance."

> "b Ceftriaxone or cefotaxime are alternative options. The choice can be made based on local availabilities."

> "c Gentamicin and amikacin are alternative options. The choice can be made based on local availabilities. In addition, amikacin (and to a lesser extent gentamicin) is still effective against many isolates producing ESBL and is considered an appropriate carbapenem-sparing option in settings where ESBL-producing isolates are very prevalent."

> "Note. All dosages are for normal renal and hepatic function. Dose adjustments may be required in patients with septic shock."

### Odak Şüphesi Varsa Aynı Tablonun Diğer Satırları (Birebir)

Menenjit (süre "10 days"):

> "First choice: Cefotaxime (IV): 2 g given every 6 hours OR Ceftriaxone (IV): 2 g given every 12 hours"

Karın içi enfeksiyon (süre "Generally 7 days"):

> "First choice: Cefotaxime (IV): 2 g given every 8 hours AND Metronidazole (IV): 500 mg given every 8 hours OR Ceftriaxone (IV): 2 g given once a day AND Metronidazole (IV): 500 mg given every 8 hours OR Piperacillin+tazobactam(e) (IV): 4 g + 500 mg given every 6 hours. Second choice: Meropenem(f) (IV): 2 g given every 8 hours"

> "f Meropenem should be considered only in settings with a high prevalence of ESBL-producing Enterobacterales."

İdrar yolu (süre "7 days"):

> "Cefotaxime(l) (IV): 2 g given every 8 hours AND Amikacin(k) (IV): 15 mg/kg given once a day OR Ceftriaxone(l) (IV): 2 g given once a day AND Amikacin(k) (IV): 15 mg/kg given once a day"

> "l Alternative antibiotics to consider based on local resistance data are piperacillin+tazobactam and carbapenems."

Alt solunum yolu (süre "5 days"): sefotaksim 2 g q8h veya seftriakson 2 g q24h + "Clarithromycin (IV): 500 mg given every 12 hours".

### MRSA Ve Dirençli Gram-Negatif

MRSA eklemesi tabloda yalnız "Skin and soft tissues infection" satırında var:

> "If MRSA is suspected, ADD Vancomycin (IV): 15–20 mg/kg given every 12 hours"

Kaynağı bilinmeyen sepsis satırında MRSA eklemesi YOK. Dirençli Gram-negatif için o
satırdaki tek söz yukarıdaki (c) dipnotudur (amikasin, karbapenem koruyucu seçenek).

Risk ayrımı metinde var ama rejime bağlanmamış:

> "High-risk patients: patients with major pre-existing comorbidities or immunocompromised and/or previous colonization or infection with a resistant pathogen. These patients have a higher risk of infections caused by multidrug-resistant bacteria."

### Zamanlama Ve Genel İlke

> "Intravenous antibiotic treatment should be started as soon as possible when sepsis of bacterial origin is suspected. Taking laboratory and microbiology tests or waiting for the results should not delay administration of the first dose of antibiotic treatment."

> "Sepsis of unknown origin. The treatment of this type of sepsis is based on the most probable clinical situation."

Yorum: Tablo yalnız TOPLUM KÖKENLİ sepsis içindir; hastane kökenli sepsis için rejim vermez.
Yoğun bakım / servis ayrımı yapmaz. Septik şok için ayrı rejim yoktur, yalnız doz notu vardır.

## 2. Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2021

- Künye: Evans L, Rhodes A, Alhazzani W, ve ark. Surviving sepsis campaign: international
  guidelines for management of sepsis and septic shock 2021. *Intensive Care Med.*
  2021;47(11):1181-1247. doi:10.1007/s00134-021-06506-y (eş yayın: Crit Care Med 2021).
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8486643/
- Yayın tarihi: 2021-10-02
- Bölüm: "Infection" — öneriler 12-30.

Yorum: SSC HİÇBİR ilaç adı, doz veya aralık vermez; yalnız kapsama ilkesi verir.

### Zamanlama

> "12. For adults with possible septic shock or a high likelihood for sepsis, we recommend administering antimicrobials immediately, ideally within 1 h of recognition" — Strong recommendation, low quality of evidence (Septic shock); Strong recommendation, very low quality of evidence (Sepsis without shock)

> "14. For adults with possible sepsis without shock, we suggest a time-limited course of rapid investigation and if concern for infection persists, the administration of antimicrobials within 3 h from the time when sepsis was first recognised" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

> "15. For adults with a low likelihood of infection and without shock, we suggest deferring antimicrobials while continuing to closely monitor the patient." — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

### MRSA

> "17. For adults with sepsis or septic shock at high risk of methicillin resistant staph aureus (MRSA), we recommend using empiric antimicrobials with MRSA coverage over using antimicrobials without MRSA coverage" — Best Practice statement

> "18. For adults with sepsis or septic shock at low risk of methicillin resistant staph aureus (MRSA), we suggest against using empiric antimicrobials with MRSA coverage, as compared with using antimicrobials without MRSA coverage" — Weak recommendation, low quality of evidence

Gerekçe metninden risk etkenleri:

> "Patient-related risk factors for MRSA include prior history of MRSA infection or colonisation, recent IV antibiotics, history of recurrent skin infections or chronic wounds, presence of invasive devices, haemodialysis, recent hospital admissions and severity of illness"

### Çok İlaca Dirençli (MDR) Gram-Negatif

> "19. For adults with sepsis or septic shock and high risk for multidrug resistant (MDR) organisms, we suggest using two antimicrobials with gram-negative coverage for empiric treatment over one gram-negative agent" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

> "20. For adults with sepsis or septic shock and low risk for MDR organisms, we suggest against using two Gram-negative agents for empiric treatment, as compared to one Gram-negative agent" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

> "21. For adults with sepsis or septic shock, we suggest against using double gram-negative coverage once the causative pathogen and the susceptibilities are known" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

Gerekçe metninden (şok vurgusu):

> "Empiric double coverage of gram-negative bacilli is most important in patients at high risk for resistant organisms with severe illness, particularly septic shock."

### Antifungal

> "22. For adults with sepsis or septic shock at high risk of fungal infection, we suggest using empiric antifungal therapy over no antifungal therapy" — Weak recommendation, low quality of evidence

> "23. For adults with sepsis or septic shock at low risk of fungal infection, we suggest against empiric use of antifungal therapy" — Weak recommendation, low quality of evidence

### Uygulama, Daraltma, Süre

> "25. For adults with sepsis or septic shock, we suggest using prolonged infusion of beta-lactams for maintenance (after an initial bolus) over conventional bolus infusion" — Weak recommendation, moderate quality of evidence

> "29. For adults with sepsis or septic shock, we suggest daily assessment for de-escalation of antimicrobials over using fixed durations of therapy without daily reassessment for de-escalation" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

> "30. For adults with an initial diagnosis of sepsis or septic shock and adequate source control, we suggest using shorter over longer duration of antimicrobial therapy" — Weak recommendation, very low quality of evidence

Yorum: SSC gün sayısı vermez. Öneri 13, 16, 24, 26-28 burada alıntılanmadı (rejim seçimiyle ilgisiz).

## 3. Türkçe / Ulusal Kaynak

### 3a. Ulusal Dernek Kılavuzu

### 3b. Hacettepe Üniversitesi (Kurumsal Algoritma, Ulusal Kılavuz Değil)

- Künye: Hacettepe University Faculty of Medicine, Department of Infectious Diseases and
  Clinical Microbiology. "1. Sepsis and Septic Shock" tanı-tedavi algoritmaları.
- URL (dizin): https://enfeksiyon.hacettepe.edu.tr/en/menu/1_sepsis_and_septic_shock-97
- 1.2: https://fs.hacettepe.edu.tr/enfeksiyon/Sepsis%20eng/Initial%20Empirical%20Treatment%20of%20the%20Patient%20in%20Sepsis%20or%20Septic%20Shock.pdf
- 1.3: https://fs.hacettepe.edu.tr/enfeksiyon/Sepsis%20eng/Initial%20Empirical%20Treatment%20of%20the%20Patient%20in%20Sepsis%20or%20Septic%20Shock%20with%20Low%20MDR%20Risk.pdf
- Tarih: sayfa altında "2023" yazıyor; algoritmaların kendi sürüm/tarihi doğrulanamadı.
- Uyarı (sayfanın kendi notu, özet): algoritmalar hastanenin yerel direnç örüntüsünü
  yansıtır, başka kurumda aynen uygulanmamalıdır.
- DOZ VERMEZ. Yalnız ilaç adı verir ("All antibiotics are administered at maximum tolerated doses").

MDR risk listesi (1.2; biri bile varsa yüksek risk kolu):

> "Hospitalized > 72 h; Immunosuppressive treatment in 30 d, OR neutropenic, OR post-allo-HSCT<1 yr, OR new diagnosed & untreated hematological malignancy; > 7 d treatment with a FQ, OR 2nd/3rd generation cephalosporin, OR beta-lactam-beta-lactamase inhibitor in the last month; Renal replacement therapy; Hospitalization > 7 days in the last month; Infected or colonized with a MDR bacteria in the last 6 mo; > 7 d share of the same hospital room with a patient infected/colonized with and MDR bacteria in the last month; Nursing home resident"

Düşük MDR riski, odak "Unknown" sütunu (1.3):

> "SAM OR Ceftriaxone / cefotaxime + metronidazole (Piperacilin-tazobactam can be preferred if the patient is in shock)"

(SAM = sulbaktam-ampisilin; kaynak kendisi "Sulbactam-ampicillin (=SAM)" diye tanımlıyor.)

Yüksek MDR riski, yoğun bakımda 72 saatten AZ (1.2):

> "No history of > 7 d antibiotic(s) in 30 d: Piperacilin-tazobactam"

> "> 7 d SAM / CAM in 30 d: Piperacilin-tazobactam + amikacin"

> "> 7 d FQ / 3rd gen cephalosporin / piperacillin-tazobactam/tigecycline/carbapenem in 30 d: Meropenem/ imipenem"

> "In septic shock with a history of unknown antibiotics: Meropenem / imipenem"

> "ESBL-producing bacterial groth in the last 6 mo.: Meropenem / imipenem"

Yoğun bakımda 72 saatten uzun ("n the ICU for > 72 h" — PDF'te baş harf düşmüş): karbapenem
dirençli Gram-negatif (CR GNB) risk etkeni yoksa "Meropenem / imipenem"; varsa
"Meropenem / imipenem + polymyxine / aminoglycoside". CR GNB risk etkenleri:

> "Colonized with CR GNB in 3 mo; Infected with CR GNB in 3 mo; >3 d carbapenem use in 15 d; Septic on carbapenem treatment; Transferred from another hospital ICU after >48 h stay and antibiotic history unknown"

Not: PDF'in metin dökümünde kutuların akış okları görünmüyor; hangi kutunun hangi dala
bağlandığı yukarıda sayfa düzeninden okunmuştur — bilgi tabanına girmeden önce PDF gözle
doğrulanmalı.

Glikopeptid (MRSA) ekleme koşulu:

> "A glycopeptide should be added to the treatment regimen in a patient with septic shock if: there is a CVC >3 d in place, the exit site of the CVC is infected, a history of MRSA growth in sputum culture in 3 mo if the site of infection for sepsis is the lungs, E. faecium growth in urine culture in 3 mo if pyuria is detected in urinalysis, the presence of grade 3 or 4 infected decubitus ulcer, or the patient was hospitalized at another center for > 48 h with an unknown antibiotic history."

Zamanlama ve infüzyon:

> "The first dose of beta-lactam antibiotics should be given in 1 h, the rest as 3-h infusion."

## 4. NICE NG253

- Künye: National Institute for Health and Care Excellence. *Suspected sepsis in people aged
  16 or over: recognition, assessment and early management*. NICE guideline NG253.
- URL: https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng253 (bölüm: /chapter/Antibiotic-therapy-intravenous-fluid-and-oxygen ve /chapter/Managing-suspected-sepsis)
- Yayın: 19 November 2025; son güncelleme: 23 September 2026 (sayfadaki ibare).

Erişkinde ilaç adı VERMEZ, yerel formülere bırakır:

> "1.9.7 For people aged 18 years or over who need an empirical intravenous antimicrobial for suspected sepsis but who have no confirmed diagnosis, use an intravenous antimicrobial from the agreed local formulary and in line with local (where available) or national guidelines. [2016]"

Tek somut ilaç 16-18 yaş için:

> "1.9.5 For people aged 16 to 18 with suspected community acquired sepsis of any cause, give ceftriaxone 80 mg/kg once a day with a maximum dose of 4 g daily at any age. [2016, amended 2024]"

Zamanlama (NEWS2 risk düzeyine göre):

> "1.8.3 Give people aged 16 or over who are at high risk of severe illness or death from sepsis broad-spectrum intravenous antibiotic treatment, within 1 hour of calculating the person's NEWS2 score on initial assessment in the emergency department or on ward deterioration."

Orta risk (1.8.17): "deferring administration of a broad-spectrum antibiotic treatment for up to 3 hours"; düşük risk (1.8.23): "for up to 6 hours".

NG51 ayrıca açılmadı (NG253 onun 16 yaş üstü için yerine geçen sürümü görünüyor; bu ilişki
sayfadan doğrulanmadı).

## Yoğun Bakım / Septik Şok İle Servis Ayrımı

| Kaynak | Ayrım Yapıyor Mu | Nasıl |
|---|---|---|
| DSÖ AWaRe 2022 | Hayır | Tek rejim; yalnız "Dose adjustments may be required in patients with septic shock." Tablo toplum kökenli sepsis için. |
| SSC 2021 | Yalnız zamanlamada | Şok / yüksek olasılık: 1 saat içinde (Ö12). Şoksuz olası sepsis: 3 saat içinde (Ö14). İlaç seçimi şoka göre değil MRSA / MDR / mantar riskine göre (Ö17-23). Gerekçede çift Gram-negatif kapsamanın en çok septik şokta önemli olduğu yazıyor. |
| NICE NG253 | Yalnız zamanlamada | NEWS2 riski: yüksek 1 saat, orta 3 saate kadar, düşük 6 saate kadar. İlaç yerel formüler. |
| Hacettepe (kurumsal) | Evet | (a) Düşük MDR riskinde şok varsa piperasilin-tazobaktam "can be preferred". (b) Yüksek MDR riskinde "septic shock with a history of unknown antibiotics" → meropenem / imipenem. (c) Yoğun bakımda > 72 saat → karbapenem tabanı, CR GNB riski varsa + polimiksin / aminoglikozid. (d) Glikopeptid yalnız septik şokta ve sayılan koşullarda. |

Yorum: Yoğun bakım / şok için rejim BASAMAĞI değiştiren tek dayanak kurumsal bir algoritmadır
(Hacettepe) ve kendi notuna göre başka kuruma aynen taşınmamalıdır. Birincil kılavuzlar (DSÖ,
SSC, NICE) şoku ilaç seçimine değil, başlama süresine ve risk değerlendirmesine bağlar.
